# IC014: an audit of SING's null-space leakage score

This repo holds the code, committed result tables and the paper (`paper_workshop/main.tex`, TMLR format; `main.pdf`) auditing SING (arXiv 2603.14610, CVPR 2026).

**SING's cross-model leakage ranking is indistinguishable from a null-energy ranking.** The null-energy fraction ρ alone reproduces SING's 13-model order as well as SING's own score (Kendall τ = 0.82 for both; paired difference CI [−0.36, 0.29]; partial τ 0.61 both ways, so neither subsumes the other). ρ is uncentred, so it includes the mean of the null coordinates. Post hoc (`r8/posthoc/POSTHOC.md`), centred ρ gives τ 0.56 on all 13 (0.48 on E12, 0.49 on S11), content-free surrogates at centred energy give 0.67, and the null offset accounts for 83% of AS for DeiT3-L and for ResNet50.
Interventions (rounds 3 and 4) show that AS has a path from null energy to the score that carries no image-specific content. They cannot separate null energy from real null content as the source of the agreement. Content-free surrogates with each model's null energy reproduce SING's order (τ 0.49–0.72 over five surrogates, all 13 models), and so does the excess of real over surrogate AS (τ 0.62). The data cannot apportion the order between the two. AS per unit null energy (A1) loses the agreement with SING's released translators (τ −0.03 / 0.09 on E12 / S11) but keeps it with retrained ridge translators (τ 0.61 / 0.75, the level of the round-3 KILL-R3 rule). Shuffling class means across classes, which preserves energy exactly, keeps the agreement (τ 0.64), which leans toward energy, with a caveat: the class-aligned part Δ_align has the stronger partial association given ρ (partial Spearman 0.76–0.88 vs 0.22–0.35), and Δ_align fails its own calibration. The pre-registration allows no stronger claim than these raw numbers. In the semi-synthetic calibration worlds (corrected in round 5, with residuals permuted across images), no estimator meets all three criteria. The translator-free NCM_null tracks content in world D on all three bases and in world M on two of three. As an external reader pointed out, the invariance (B), energy (C) and content (M) criteria cannot all be met by any score of the null and row energies, since B leaves only their ratio and C and M both move only that ratio; world C also holds content fixed per unit of total energy, so content rises 1.81× against the row space.
Round 5 (`PREREG_R5.md`) tested a pre-registered, translator-free score of how the null class means are organised relative to the class texts (SO_null, with XCC_24 as a secondary score). It is invariant to rescaling the null part. Both scores pass their invariance, derangement and planted-code gates. The energy gate in the corrected world C was testable for SO_null on only one of three bases. No kill fired. Against SING's order, SO_null gives τ 0.33 [−0.29, 0.79] / 0.45 [−0.21, 0.88] on E12 / S11 (INCONCLUSIVE), and XCC_24 gives −0.12 / −0.05 (DOES-NOT-TRACK).
Round 6 (`PREREG_R6.md`, pushed with its code and synthetic unit tests before any real-model number) applied new energy statistics to four published null-space or residual scores (ViM, ActSub S→, CORE S_mem, conditional V-information). None is ENERGY-DRIVEN (k = 0 of n = 4). Post hoc positive controls, however, show that the rule flags SING's IS and misses SING's AS (released: τ_EQ 0.39 / 0.45, median R_B 0.26, MIXED), ρ and the NuSA share. The count therefore says nothing about whether the confound is specific to SING. ViM, ActSub and CORE keep their order under equal energy largely because they are scale-normalised (ViM with α frozen: ResNet50 0.876 → 2.5e-4). The retained cross-model range after equalising energy is 0.11 for SING's AS, 0.29 for V-information, 0.78 for ActSub, 0.90 for ViM and 0.92 for CORE. It is a descriptive comparison, not a validity gate and not the fraction of confounding removed: ViM with α frozen retains 1.93 of its range yet is ENERGY-DRIVEN. The scores are surrogates fitted on 5 images per class. The content tests cannot separate a loss of image-level information from estimator noise. See `r6/README.md` and `r6/POSTHOC_R6.md`. A post hoc rerun of the round-5 SO_null with exact centring changes no outcome (`r5/README.md`).
Round 7 (`r7/PREREG_R7.md`) tested controlled background edits on IN-9 under independent nuisance views on four models: the edit is reproducible (H1 4/4), but a pixel-permuted artefact control aligns with the inserted class text as strongly (H2 ARTEFACT 4/4), so H3 was not tested and the family statement is NOT SUPPORTED. The registered bootstrap was invalid for these U-statistics; a post hoc half-sampling fix changes no verdict. The edits test a different construct from SING's. See `r7/README.md`.
Round 8 (`r8/PREREG_R8.md`) stated a target first (T1, the share of text-predictable class signal in ker W) and a score θ_sem. G-noise and G-derange failed (6 of 8 gates pass), so θ_sem is NOT VALIDATED and its τ 0.636 with SING's order is reported, not read; the world-C′ gate was low-powered. Descriptively, the shadow of the null class means predicted from the row space tracks SING's order (τ 0.76 on E12) and the innovation does not (0.21); θ_sem's partial correlation with the order given content-free energy is −0.41 [−0.85, 0.18]; the head's self-duality gives τ −0.64; and refitting the head (NCM, shrinkage LDA) moves ρ and θ_sem by orders of magnitude. See `r8/README.md` and `r8/posthoc/POSTHOC.md`.
Other results: two true DINO models rank 8th and 9th of 15 by AS, not lowest; SING's "DinoViT" translator matches the supervised timm AugReg ViT-L/16. Size-corrected rankings differ from SING's but do not reverse it with confidence. Every pre-registered outcome is reported, including failures; see `RESULTS.md` and the ledgers in the paper appendix.

* `e1/` energy law; `e2/` 13-model ranking and claim audit; `e3/` planted-concept sensitivity; `e8/` null concept probe (pre-registered, KILL); `e9/` score screen (pre-registered, KILL).
* `r2/` round-2 analyses (`PREREG_R2.md`, `ROUND2_PLAN.md`); outputs in `r2/results/`. See `r2/README.md`.
* `r3/` round-3 analyses (`PREREG_R3.md`, `ROUND3_PLAN.md`): equal-energy interventions, leak-free null, retrained translators, PC bands vs the energy law, paired statistics, table generation. See `r3/README.md`.
* `r4/` round-4 analyses (`PREREG_R4.md`, `PREREG_R4B.md`, `PREREG_R4C.md`, `ROUND4_PLAN.md`): surrogates, A1 statistics, retrained A4, planted content, true DINO, class-mean permutation, calibration worlds, selectivity, NCM_null, NZS, geometry. See `r4/README.md`.
* `r5/` round-5 analyses (`PREREG_R5.md`): calibration-world correction (old vs new), organisation scores SO_null and XCC_24 with gates G1–G5, confirmatory τ and the inheritance diagnostic. See `r5/README.md`.
* `r6/` round-6 audit of published null/residual scores (`PREREG_R6.md`): ViM, ActSub S→, CORE S_mem, conditional V-information and a NuSA-style reference under equal-energy, energy-sweep and content tests; frozen synthetic unit tests in `r6/tests/`; post hoc positive controls and sensitivity checks (`r6/POSTHOC_R6.md`). See `r6/README.md`.
* `r7/` round-7 background-edit test (`r7/PREREG_R7.md`): IN-9 edits, artefact and nuisance controls, independent views, T2 alignment, post hoc bootstrap correction (`r7/sens_r7.py`). See `r7/README.md`.
* `r8/` round-8 target-first score θ_sem (`r8/PREREG_R8.md`): gates, worlds C′/M/D, shadow/innovation, self-duality and head swap; `r8/posthoc/` holds the post hoc ρ decomposition and offset analyses (`r8/posthoc/POSTHOC.md`). See `r8/README.md`.
* `paper_workshop/tables/` LaTeX tables and number macros generated by `r3/make_tables.py`, `r4/make_tables_r4.py`, `r5/make_tables_r5.py`, `r6/make_tables_r6.py`, `r6/make_tables_r6_posthoc.py` and `r8/make_tables_r8.py` (rounds 7 and 8 and the offset analysis; do not edit by hand).
* `gpu_jobs/` specs for work that needs ImageNet images or a GPU.
* `paper_workshop/archive/` earlier drafts.

## Reproduce
Environment: `python -m venv v && v/bin/pip install -r requirements-pinned.txt` (exact versions of the direct dependencies, CPU torch, Python 3.13; checked in a fresh venv with `scripts/reproduce.sh`). `requirements.txt` gives looser ranges; `requirements-lock.txt` is the old whole-machine freeze, kept for reference only.

* `make reproduce` (or `scripts/reproduce.sh`) regenerates every table, figure and number macro in the paper from **committed aggregate results** (round-2 summaries, `r3/results/F_paired_stats.json`, round-4, round-5, round-6 and round-7/8 macros and tables, `r6/results/R6_summary.json`, `r6/results/R6_posthoc.json`, `r5/results/posthoc/SO_exact_summary.json`, `paper_workshop/figs/`, `paper_workshop/tables/`, `paper_workshop/holdout_numbers.tex`). CPU, about 1 min. This checks consistency from aggregates only; it does not recompute anything from features. In a clean clone, `bash scripts/reproduce.sh && git diff --exit-code` passes.
* `make reproduce-r4 IC014_FEATS=/path/to/feats` reruns the round-4 feature-level analyses (see `r4/README.md`); the true DINO features come from a GPU extraction.
* `make reproduce-r5 IC014_FEATS=/path/to/feats` reruns the round-5 analyses (corrected calibration worlds, about 20 min on 4 cores; organisation-score gates and confirmatory test, about 10 min on 4 cores; see `r5/README.md`).
* `make reproduce-r6 IC014_FEATS=/path/to/feats` reruns the round-6 audit (unit tests, 13 models and three calibration bases; about 2.5 h wall with four 2-thread processes on 8 shared cores; see `r6/README.md`), followed by `make reproduce-r6-posthoc` (SING AS/IS and frozen-α ViM controls, V-information penalty rerun; `r6/POSTHOC_R6.md`).
* `make paper` builds the PDF with tectonic (`tectonic -X compile main.tex`).
* `make reproduce-full IC014_FEATS=/path/to/feats` reruns the feature-level analyses (rounds 2 and 3). It needs the private ImageNet features (checked by `scripts/verify_feats.sh` against `scripts/checksums.sha256`) and SING at commit e47b37d (`scripts/fetch_sing.sh`). There is no default feature path; a missing `IC014_FEATS` stops with an error. Per-image private outputs go to `IC014_PRIVATE` (default: the git-ignored `_private/`), never to the feature store.
  * Wall time on 8 CPU cores: a little over 2 h, most of it the E9 holdout (full run 1.95 h, about 7000 s); everything else takes about 15 min.
  * `make holdout-fast IC014_FEATS=...` runs the reduced holdout declared in `PREREG_R3.md` (N10): 5 draws instead of 20, 2 subspaces instead of 5, S9 dropped; resumable from its cache (checked: a run restarted from 40 of 64 cached cells skips them and ends with an identical cache and results). Timed on 8 cores: 36.3 min (target was under 30 min, missed; the machine was shared with other jobs, load not recorded, and it was not re-timed in round 4). M1 and AS_SING Kendall means and all flatness verdicts are identical to the full run; AS_fair differs by +0.024 (linear) and +0.057 (nonlinear), and M1 − AS_fair by −0.024 / −0.057, so the nonlinear values miss the ±0.03 target. Use the full run for the paper numbers.
* **GPU work** (feature extraction; needs ILSVRC2012-val): two jobs were timed, Exp 1 features (1.4 GPU-h, one cloud GPU) and the fix-14 ViT-L/16 re-extraction (1.9 h on 2× T4 ≈ 3.8 GPU-h including weight downloads). The other extraction jobs were not timed; we estimate under 10 GPU-h in total. Specs are in `gpu_jobs/` (including the not-yet-run `gpu_jobs/r3_dino_vitb16.md`).

## Data
We do not redistribute ImageNet images or features derived from them (ImageNet terms of access).
The image lists are in `scripts/subset_files_10k.txt` (10 per class, file name and label) and `scripts/subset_files_claims200.txt`. Re-extracting the features requires your own copy of ILSVRC2012 val.
